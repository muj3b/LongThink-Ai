To find the product of \(37 \times 29\), we can use the distributive property to simplify the calculation step by step.

**Step 1:** Break down 29 into 20 + 9.

\[
37 \times 29 = 37 \times (20 + 9)
\]

**Step 2:** Distribute the multiplication over addition.

\[
= 37 \times 20 + 37 \times 9
\]

**Step 3:** Calculate each part separately.

\[
37 \times 20 = 740
\]
\[
37 \times 9 = 333
\]

**Step 4:** Add the two results together.

\[
740 + 333 = 1073
\]

Therefore, \(37 \times 29\) equals \(\boxed{1073}\).
